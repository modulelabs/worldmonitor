# The Next Production Cycle

The Next Production Cycle is a decision platform for the shift from labour-and-capital production to capability that stays inside a biophysical floor.

Forecast explores scenario relationships. Live is the geographic monitor. Missing series stay blank.

**Public app:** [thenextproductioncycle.vercel.app](https://thenextproductioncycle.vercel.app)

## Product

Production is moving from labour and capital toward capability. The platform is for deciding how that shift is owned, financed, and bounded.

Five objects carry the decision:

| Object | Role |
|--------|------|
| Human Horizon | Ownership, reduced labour, and agency |
| Capital → Capability | Capital directed into capability |
| Develop / Optimise / Preserve | The three vectors of the cycle |
| Useful Societal Capability | The outcome |
| Biophysical Floor | The hard bound |

Paper phases: 2026–2029 structural realignment, 2030–2034 spatial refactoring, 2035–2040 biophysical equilibrium.

Forecast numbers are scenario math. They are not a cited empirical series. A blank field means the series has not been ingested. It is not zero.

## Platform

- **Forecast** explores relationships among ownership, capital-to-capability, Develop / Optimise / Preserve, and the biophysical floor.
- **Live** is the city spatial monitor. Map signals and overlays follow the five Product objects (Spatial Cycle): Human Horizon → Capital → Capability → Develop / Optimise / Preserve → Useful Societal Capability → Biophysical Floor. Needs, capital, and location series stay empty until a cited source is ingested.
- The public map is the Live geographic monitor: [thenextproductioncycle.vercel.app](https://thenextproductioncycle.vercel.app).
